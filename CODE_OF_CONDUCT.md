# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What is expected

- **Argue with the claim, not the person.** "This measurement is wrong because X" is welcome. "You clearly did not think about this" is not.
- **Say what you actually know.** If something is a guess, a projection, or an untested assumption, label it. This project has already shipped one wrong number because it was estimated rather than measured; catching that sort of thing is a contribution.
- **Accept a correction gracefully.** Being wrong in public is the ordinary cost of working in public.
- **Respect the maintainer's time and your own.** Nobody here is owed a review, a merge, or a reply on a schedule.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwelcome attention, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else this project is represented.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/WDahah). Reports are handled discreetly.

The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete contributions that do not follow this document, and may block accounts for repeated or severe behaviour. Enforcement is a judgement call and will not always be consistent; it will try to be fair.

## Security issues

Vulnerabilities go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.
